01 / MANIFESTO

Why this exists

The internet is full of clean templates. This is the opposite: a site with attitude, tension, and a slightly unruly rhythm.

Offgrid Atelier designs launch moments that feel authored, not assembled. Every project gets a distinct visual grammar, a mood with teeth, and a pacing system that refuses sameness.

Think: cultural campaigns, fashion capsules, music releases, exhibition identities, and digital portfolios that want to be remembered on first pass.

06 / ART DIRECTION

Notes for the designer

Use asymmetry, oversized type, and compositions that look edited by hand, not generated from a prompt.

1

Keep at least one section slightly chaotic.

2

Let images and typography overlap without apology.

3

Use motion as punctuation, not decoration.

4

Leave traces of texture, ink, and noise on the page.
